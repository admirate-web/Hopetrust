Detoxification is the first step for people who are looking for **([Hope Trust](/))**. Detoxification means a treatment for addiction to drugs or alcohol. It is intended to remove the physiological effects of the addictive substances and also neutralize the toxic properties (which normally affects the liver). Detox is required before starting the recovery program for alcohol addiction. At Hope Trust, detox is done under the care of a medical team comprising of physicians and a psychiatrist. The individual is monitored for physical and psychological symptoms during detox.

Alcohol addiction   includes all the symptoms of substance abuse. It also includes another element – physical dependence on drinking. If you consider alcohol as a necessary element to let you perform or feel physically forced to drink, then you may consider yourself  an alcoholic.

Treating alcoholism may require advanced treatments which may be a bit complex. So for the treatment to work, the alcoholic should practice sobriety. However, usually, you can’t force an individual to abstain from drinking or think about treatment if they aren’t prepared. Success depends on the individual’s personal drive. The recovery method for an alcoholic could be a lifetime commitment. It involves daily maintenance and isn’t a quick fix. For this reason, many of us say **alcohol addiction** is rarely “cured.” It is, however, “treatable”.

An alcoholic may benefit from different treatment strategies like medical care, counselling, or nutrition related treatments. A doctor may also prescribe antidepressants if the alcoholic suffers from depression. Additionally, different type of medicine may also be prescribed for recovery from various emotional traumas. An expert is beneficial in teaching somebody to manage the strain of recovery and also the skills required to stop relapse. A healthy diet will further helpthe person to recover his health as well as control their weight gain or loss.

In general, **alcohol addiction** involves a versatile range of different treatments that can vary from one person to another. It is necessary that the alcoholic opts for a recovery program that will work for him or her. The correct treatment for you depends on your specific needs.
