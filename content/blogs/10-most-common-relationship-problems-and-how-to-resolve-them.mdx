Here are some typical relationship problems and solutions:

### 1. Lack of trust

Trust is essential for any healthy relationship. If you or your partner is experiencing trust issues, it’s critical to have an open and honest discussion about what’s causing the mistrust. Consider seeing a professional therapist to ([How Couples Can Rebuild Trust in Their Relationship](/blogs/how-couples-can-rebuild-trust-in-their-relationship/))
### 2. Poor communication

Communication is essential for a healthy relationship. If you’re having trouble communicating with your partner, try being more aware of your tone and body language. Ensure you’re actively listening to your partner rather than waiting for your turn to speak. If you’re still struggling, consider couples therapy.
### 3. Financial disagreements

Money can be a significant source of conflict in a relationship. If you and your partner disagree about money, try to have an open and honest discussion about your financial goals and priorities. Consider creating a budget together and finding ways to compromise.
### 4. Jealousy

Jealousy can indicate insecurity or a loss of trust in a relationship. If you are feeling jealous, try to pinpoint the source of your jealousy. Is there a need for more confidence in your partner? Do you feel insecure? After identifying the cause, communicate your feelings openly and honestly with your partner.
### 5. Different priorities

Finding common ground with your partner can be difficult. Try to have an open and honest discussion about your priorities and find areas for compromise. Remember that having different priorities is okay if you’re willing to collaborate to find a solution.
### 6. Lack of intimacy

([8 Ways to Deepen Love, According to Experts](/blogs/8-ways-to-deepen-love-according-to-experts/)). If you’re feeling disconnected from your partner, try to pinpoint the source of the issue. Is there a lack of physical intimacy? Emotional intimacy? Once you’ve determined the cause, talk openly and honestly with your partner about your feelings. If you continue to struggle, ([our therapists](/book-your-session/)).
### 7. Different values

Finding common ground with your partner can be difficult if your values differ. Try to have an open and honest conversation about your values and find areas for compromise. Remember that having different values is okay if you’re willing to collaborate to find a solution.
### 8. Lack of quality time

Spending quality time together is essential in any relationship. If you and your partner have trouble finding time to spend together, try prioritising it. Consider regular date nights or other ways to connect, such as going for a walk or cooking together.
### 9. External stressors

Several external factors can spill over into a relationship and contribute to problems. Some examples of these include stress at work, problems with family, and health problems.
### 10. Unrealistic expectations

When a person expects their partner to meet all of their emotional, physical, and social needs, it can lead to feelings of disappointment and dissatisfaction.

Remember that each relationship is unique, and what works for one couple might not work for another. If you’re having relationship problems, don’t hesitate to see a professional therapist. ([our mental health services](/mental-health/)).

## How to resolve relationship issues

Hope Trust relationship counsellors suggest implementing any or all of the following that will benefit any relationship:

-   Say, please, and thank you
-   Express your dissatisfaction without using profanity or name-calling
-   Plan regular date nights, even during the day or at home
-   Be proactive in pleasing your partner sexually
-   Spend time asking each other about their wants and needs
-   Take time out from debates that are turning into arguments

_Here are more strategies to bring the magic back into your relationship:_

-   Remember what first drew you to your partner
-   Ask yourself where the relationship problem comes from, and then take steps to improve it
-   Be honest with your partner and enjoy rediscovering the excitement of your relationship
